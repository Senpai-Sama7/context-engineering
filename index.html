Part 8

Common Mistakes

Learn from what everyone gets wrong.

Mistake

"Make me an app"

Why it fails

Zero context — AI guesses everything

The fix

Use the CONTEXT framework

Mistake

"Make it look nice"

Why it fails

"Nice" means nothing

The fix

Name a real app as reference

Mistake

"Add all the features"

Why it fails

Scope explosion

The fix

Define the ONE thing it must do

Mistake

"Just like Uber but for X"

Why it fails

Too vague — Uber has 1000+ features

The fix

"Like Uber's map screen but for X"

Mistake

"Make it responsive"

Why it fails

Technical jargon you don't need

The fix

"Must work on my phone"

Mistake

No test case

Why it fails

You can't tell if it actually works

The fix

"I'll know it works when..."

Mistake

Forgetting empty states

Why it fails

Beautiful with data, broken without

The fix

"When there's nothing there yet, show..."

Mistake

Asking for everything at once

Why it fails

AI spreads thin, does nothing well

The fix

One feature at a time, verify, then add more

Mistake

"Fix the bug" without context

Why it fails

AI can't reproduce it

The fix

Describe exactly what you did, saw, and expected

Mistake

Not saying what you DON'T want

Why it fails

AI fills in blanks with "obvious" defaults

The fix

"Never [thing]" is as powerful as "Make [thing]"
